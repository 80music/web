## Platform

web

## Users
Musicians, piano and guitar students, hobbyists, music teachers, and curious listeners who want to instantly know what chords are being played in real life and train their musical ear without tedious theory textbooks.

## Product Purpose
Chordisol is a native iOS companion app that listens to acoustic instruments (pianos, acoustic guitars, ukuleles, voice) via the device microphone. It isolates polyphonic frequencies and identifies chords in real time, then turns music theory into gamified, bite-sized song puzzles with cloud-synced progress. This website is the official marketing and educational landing page promoting the iOS app.

## Positioning
Unlike web-based DAWs or desktop chord sequencers that require MIDI cables, audio interfaces, and complex setup, Chordisol turns an everyday iPhone into an attentive practice partner. Prop the phone by your instrument, play naturally, and see the chords appear instantly on-screen with progress synced to your account.

## Operating Context
Users practice in living rooms, practice rooms, bedrooms, classrooms, and stage rehearsal spaces. They have real physical instruments in front of them (a piano, keyboard, guitar, or ukulele) and want hands-free, glanceable visual chord feedback while their hands stay on their instrument.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Multi-instrument acoustic detection: Piano & Keyboards (88 keys), Acoustic & Electric Guitar (6 strings), Ukulele (4 strings), and Voice/Humming.
- Low-latency Core Audio analysis running on Apple Silicon.
- Account Sign-in & Progress Sync: log in to save and resume song puzzles, practice history, and ear training progress.
- 5 Gamified Vinyl Ear Training modes: Trust Your Ear, Which is Good?, Fill the Gap, Stack Stack, Swap the Notes.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: Chordisol
- Personality: Warm, playful, tactile, encouraging, human (like Duolingo and Headspace, combined with Flighty's native iOS polish).
- Mascots: The character family created in Figma (Radio Note, Hill Note with C block, Question Note, Compare Notes).
- Aesthetics: High-contrast typography, tactile physical card borders, warm joyful color palette, zero generic AI slop.
